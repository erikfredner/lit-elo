The American Canon According to LMs

What do language models (LMs) think the American literary canon is? This project presents four LMs from different makers with a choice between two authors or two works, and asks each to judge which is more canonical as if it were a professor of American literature. Rankings on the Elo scale emerge from hundreds of thousands of pairwise comparisons, pooled across all four models and also shown for each model on its own.
